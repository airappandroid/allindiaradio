Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AIR Radio App

Is the app completely free?

Yes, all radio stations and features are 100% free to access. The app is supported by unobtrusive Google AdMob ads.

Does the audio play when screen is locked?

Yes, our app is built with Android foreground audio services so you can listen uninterrupted with your phone locked.

Do I need high-speed internet?

No, the audio streams are compressed with low-latency codecs, allowing smooth playback even on normal 3G or 4G data speeds.

Who owns the radio channels and music?

All channels, audio content, and logos belong to their respective broadcasters (such as Prasar Bharati / All India Radio). Our app functions strictly as a stream player.
