Privacy Policy

Tip Calculator – Daily Tools
Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tip Calculator – Daily Tools (“the App”) handles information when you use it. We aim to keep the App simple and transparent.

1. Information We Handle

The App does not require you to create an account. The calculator values you enter, such as bill amounts, tip percentages, tax amounts, discounts, working hours, and pay rates, are used to perform calculations on your device.

The App may store limited preferences and recent calculation history locally on your device so that features such as your selected theme and recent calculations can work. This locally stored information is not sent to our servers by the App.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. If advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, app interactions, and advertising data, as permitted by your device settings, consent choices, and applicable law. This information may be used to provide, measure, and improve advertising, including personalized or non-personalized ads as applicable.

Third-party services process information under their own privacy policies. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about how AdMob uses data.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by the App is used to provide calculator functions, remember local preferences, show recent calculations, and support advertising if ads are enabled. We do not sell the calculation values you enter.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Recent calculations and app preferences may remain in the App's local storage until you clear the App's data, uninstall the App, or the information is otherwise removed by your device or the App. Advertising-related data may be retained by third-party providers according to their policies and settings.

5. Your Choices

  • You can clear the App's locally stored data through your device settings.
  • You can manage advertising and privacy choices through the controls available on your device and, where provided, the App's consent options.
  • You can limit or reset your device's advertising identifier through your device settings, where supported.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose calculator and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the concern.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the App simple and protect information handled by it. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Third-party advertising services are responsible for the security practices applicable to information they process.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Your continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
