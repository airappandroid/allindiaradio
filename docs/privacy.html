Privacy Policy

App: AIR Radio App | Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AIR Radio App ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you use our Android mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining the confidentiality of any information related to your use of our service.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We prioritize your privacy above all. AIR Radio App does not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (PII), including but not limited to:

  • Your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • Precise or approximate physical GPS location.
  • Contacts, media, files, camera, or microphone access.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep this application completely free, we display third-party advertisements through Google AdMob. These third-party services may automatically collect limited technical and non-personally identifiable data to optimize and personalize advertisements.

Data Collected by AdMob / Google Play Services:
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and mobile network operator.
  • Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) to measure ad engagement and limit ad repetition.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs and app performance statistics to troubleshoot errors.

To learn how Google utilizes and protects data collected through AdMob, please visit:
Google Advertising & Privacy Policy | Google Privacy Policy

3. Android Permissions Required

The application only requests permissions strictly necessary to deliver radio streaming functionality:

  • INTERNET: Essential to connect to audio servers and stream live radio broadcasts.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the app to verify active network connectivity (Wi-Fi or cellular) before attempting to stream audio.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Allows seamless audio streaming in the background while you navigate other apps or lock your screen.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Displays playback status and audio controls (play/pause/stop) in your notification bar and lock screen.

4. Content & Copyright Disclaimer

AIR Radio App is an independent streaming aggregator / media player.

  • All audio feeds, channel names, trademarks, and logos belong exclusively to their respective owners and broadcasters (including Prasar Bharati / All India Radio).
  • We do not host, store, re-encode, or alter any broadcast stream on our own servers. All content is accessed directly via publicly available stream URLs.
  • If you are a copyright holder or broadcaster and wish to have your channel added, modified, or removed, please reach out to us via our contact information below.

5. Children's Privacy

Our service does not knowingly target or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through third-party services within our app, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be reflected immediately by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document. Users are advised to review this policy periodically for any changes.
